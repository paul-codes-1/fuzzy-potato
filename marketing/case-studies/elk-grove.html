The Challenge

Elk Grove is a fast-growing city south of Sacramento with a population of 176,000. Its City Council, Planning Commission, and six advisory bodies hold over 120 public meetings per year, each generating 1-3 hours of recorded video hosted on Granicus.

The City Clerk's office faced a compounding problem:

3+ hrs

Per meeting: staff time to review video, prepare draft minutes, extract vote counts, and publish summaries

2-3 weeks

Average delay between a meeting and published minutes, creating a public transparency gap

0%

Of historical meeting content was text-searchable — residents had to watch full videos to find specific discussions

40+

FOIA/public records requests per month related to meeting content that staff had to manually fulfill

"Our clerks are talented people spending their expertise on transcription grunt work," one staff member noted. "Meanwhile, residents call us asking what happened at last Tuesday's meeting and we tell them to watch a two-hour video. That's not real transparency."

The Solution

Elk Grove deployed CivicLens Professional, connecting it directly to their existing Granicus instance. The system began processing meetings automatically as they were published.

What CivicLens automates for Elk Grove

AI Transcription with Timestamps

Every meeting is transcribed at 98% accuracy with segment-level timestamps, enabling click-to-seek from any point in the summary to the exact moment in the Granicus video.

Two-Pass Structured Fact Extraction

Pass 1 (GPT-4o) extracts every vote, roll call, dollar amount, appointment, and public comment into structured JSON. Pass 2 (Claude Sonnet) generates a section-by-section narrative summary. No hallucinated data.

Searchable Archive + Natural Language Q&A

Full-text search across all meetings. Residents and staff can ask questions in plain English and get sourced answers with timestamps citing the exact meeting and moment.

Agenda + Minutes Cross-Referencing

Agendas and official minutes are automatically downloaded, OCR'd if scanned, and cross-referenced with the transcript. Everything in one place per meeting.
