Competitive Landscape

Only player in the upper-right quadrant

CivicLens

Full pipeline: transcription + structured extraction + narrative summary + cross-archive Q&A. Purpose-built for Granicus ecosystem.

Granicus

Incumbent with deep relationships and 70% market share. Platform company, not an AI company. Incentive to partner, not compete.

General AI Tools (Otter, Fireflies, Rev)

Transcription-focused or consumer-oriented. No structured civic data extraction, no vote tracking, no government-specific features.

Moat: No existing solution combines Granicus-native ingestion, structured civic extraction, narrative AI, and cross-archive Q&A. Building all four simultaneously is a 12-month head start.

Technology

Multi-model AI pipeline, built for scale

Scalability

Processing is embarrassingly parallel. Each meeting is independent. Scales horizontally via Lambda/ECS.

Multi-Model

Each AI model used for its strength. Whisper for audio, GPT-4o for extraction, Claude for narration. No single-vendor lock-in.

Cost Efficiency

$0.50/meeting at scale. Caching + batching reduces marginal costs 40%. API costs declining 15%+ annually.

Security Path
