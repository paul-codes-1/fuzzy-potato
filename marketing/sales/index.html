Non-negotiable

Rules of engagement

  1. 1.
    Never disparage. Granicus, Legistar, Novus, Swagit — treat every incumbent as a respected tool with legitimate strengths. Local government is a small world.
  2. 2.
    Lead with customer pain. The clerk's 4-hour summary write-up matters more than any feature-comparison table.
  3. 3.
    Cite or qualify. Every specific claim about a competitor must be traceable to a public source. If you don't know, say "I'm not sure — let me check."
  4. 4.
    Honest about what we don't have. SOC 2 is in progress, not attested. We are not FedRAMP. Say it out loud. Losing a deal honestly beats winning a customer who bounces on compliance review.
  5. 5.
    Disqualify fast. If the fit isn't there, say so in the first call. Your time and theirs is the resource we can never get back.
