1. Service Description

CivicLens provides a cloud-based software-as-a-service ("SaaS") platform that enables local governments, municipalities, and authorized organizations to:

  • Automatically download, transcribe, and summarize public meeting recordings from Granicus and other government media platforms
  • Generate structured, searchable archives of meeting content including agendas, minutes, votes, and financial actions
  • Provide natural-language question-and-answer capabilities over meeting archives via retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
  • Publish a public-facing web interface for constituents to browse and search meeting records
  • Access meeting data programmatically via authenticated REST APIs
