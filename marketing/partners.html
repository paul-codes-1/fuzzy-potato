Deal registration, done right

Nobody wants to source a deal and then watch their commission disappear because another partner — or worse, our own team — came in from a different angle. Deal reg protects you.

When you register an opportunity through the partner portal, it is locked to you for 90 days (renewable). If the deal closes in that window, the commission is yours. We do not accept conflicting registrations on the same account for the same scope, and our direct sales team is instructed to hand off registered accounts back to the partner of record.

Start registering deals

    Register the opportunity
    Submit account name, contact, estimated ARR, and expected close date through the portal.
  • 2

    Either approved (locked to you for 90 days) or declined with a reason — usually a prior registration.
  • 3

Frequently asked questions

The things partners ask us before signing. Straight answers.

Can I sell my own implementation services on top?
Yes, absolutely — and we encourage it. Your services revenue is yours to keep at 100%. CivicLens is a product, not a services business. You can bill your own implementation, training, change management, or custom integration hours however you want, and the platform rev share is on top of that.
Do you compete with your partners on services?
No. CivicLens is a product-only company. We do managed SaaS ops, product support, and engineering — we do not compete for consulting, change management, integration, or training dollars. If a customer asks us for services work, our default is to refer them to a qualified partner.
What is the minimum commitment?
None at Referral tier — no quota, no minimum deals. Solution tier carries a soft expectation of at least one closed deal per year to remain active (we will warn you well before deactivation). Strategic tier commitments are negotiated case by case during the partnership review.
Can I white-label the platform?
White-labeling is available as a Strategic Partner / Enterprise tier option. We provide per-tenant branding (logo, colors, custom domain) to every customer at no extra cost, but a full OEM arrangement — where the product is sold under your brand with no CivicLens attribution — requires a Strategic Partner agreement and negotiated terms.
How fast do commissions pay out?
Net 30 after the customer pays us. If a customer pays an annual invoice on March 1, your commission clears by March 31. Monthly invoicing follows the same rhythm. We send a statement at the end of every month showing all registered deals, booked revenue, and commission owed. Direct deposit is standard; check is available if you prefer.
What if a customer I sourced churns?
Commission is paid on collected revenue, so if a customer churns mid-term, future commission stops. We do not claw back commission already paid out. We will also proactively notify you of any at-risk accounts so you can help with renewal or expansion.
Do you accept international partners?
CivicLens is US-focused today because our data pipeline is optimized for Granicus, Legistar, and US municipal government workflows. We are open to international partnerships on a case-by-case basis, particularly in Canada, the UK, Ireland, Australia, and New Zealand where meeting formats are similar. Contact us to discuss.
