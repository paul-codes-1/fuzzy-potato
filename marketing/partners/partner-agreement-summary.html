Revenue share calculation

Your commission is calculated on Net Paid ARR — the subscription revenue CivicLens actually collects from a customer you sourced, after refunds, credits, and chargebacks.

  • Rate by tier: 15% (Referral), 25% (Solution), or 30%+ (Strategic) of Net Paid ARR.
  • Duration: Commission applies to the first 12 months of the customer relationship. Strategic Partners may negotiate extended or perpetual terms during review.
  • What is excluded: Pass-through infrastructure costs on self-hosted deployments, third-party LLM API fees billed at cost, Stripe processing fees, and any services you bill directly to the customer (which are 100% yours).
  • Payment timing: Net 30 from the date CivicLens receives payment from the customer. Statements sent monthly.
  • No clawback: Commission already paid out is not clawed back if the customer later churns. Future commission stops when the customer stops paying.
  • Expansion & upsells: Commission applies to the initial subscription plus any organic expansion within the 12-month window (e.g., customer upgrades from Pro to Enterprise).

Example: You register and close a Pro plan customer at $799/mo on March 1. You are Solution Partner (25%). The customer pays monthly. Each month CivicLens collects the $799, you receive $199.75 commission Net 30. Over 12 months, total commission is $2,397.

2

Deal registration rules

Deal registration is how we protect partners from conflict — both from each other and from our own direct sales team.

  • How to register: Submit account name, primary contact, estimated ARR, and expected close date through the partner portal (or by email during early-program phase).
  • Response time: CivicLens responds within 2 business days with approval or decline. Declines include a reason — typically a pre-existing registration or an active direct relationship.
  • Lock period: Approved registrations lock the account to you for 90 days. You may request a 90-day extension with a progress update.
  • Exclusivity scope: A registration protects the specific legal entity named (e.g., "City of Elk Grove") for the scope of CivicLens subscription sales. It does not block other partners from selling unrelated services to that entity.
  • First-to-register wins: If two partners register the same account, the earlier timestamp wins. Ties are resolved by material evidence of engagement (meeting notes, emails, proposals).
  • CivicLens direct sales: Our direct team is instructed to check the partner registry before any new outbound activity. If a registered account contacts us directly, we route the inquiry back to the partner of record.
3

IP ownership

Clean and simple: you own your services, we own the product.

You own

  • ✓ Your services methodology & playbooks
  • ✓ Implementation deliverables you create for clients
  • ✓ Custom scripts, dashboards, or configs you build
  • ✓ Your brand, templates, training material
  • ✓ Client data you handle under your own DPA

CivicLens owns

  • ✓ The CivicLens platform, code, and models
  • ✓ The CivicLens brand, logo, and trademarks
  • ✓ Product documentation and API schemas
  • ✓ Aggregate telemetry and product analytics
  • ✓ Feedback you share with us (to improve the product)

Feedback license: If you share ideas, feature requests, or bug reports with us, we get a perpetual license to use them to improve the product. We will never claim your proprietary methodology or client-specific work.

Integration work: If you build an integration between CivicLens and another product using our public API, you own that integration. If we jointly develop something under a Strategic Partner engagement, ownership is negotiated in a separate Statement of Work.

4

Termination

  • For convenience: Either party can terminate the partner agreement for any reason with 30 days written notice.
  • For cause: Either party can terminate immediately for material breach (non-payment, misrepresentation, violation of law, brand abuse) if the other party does not cure within 15 days of notice.
  • Commission tail: After termination, you continue to receive commission on customers you sourced through the end of their 12-month commission window. We do not claw back commission already paid, and we do not cancel future payments on already-closed deals unless the termination was for cause related to that deal.
  • Data handoff: On termination we provide a final statement of all registered accounts, pipeline status, and outstanding commission. We will not poach customers you closed during the partnership.
  • Survival: Confidentiality, IP, and payment obligations survive termination. Brand usage rights end on termination — you must remove CivicLens marks from your website and collateral within 30 days.
5

Confidentiality

Standard two-way NDA baked into the partner agreement.

  • What is confidential: Roadmap, pricing below published tiers, customer pipeline, internal architecture details, unreleased features, any information marked confidential, and anything a reasonable person would treat as confidential.
  • What is not confidential: Publicly available information, anything you already knew before the partnership, anything independently developed without use of our confidential info, and anything required by law to disclose (with notice to us if legally permitted).
  • Duration: Confidentiality obligations last for 3 years after termination of the partner agreement.
  • Customer data: Customer data you encounter during implementation is covered by the customer's own agreement with CivicLens, not this NDA. You should have your own DPA with the customer for work you perform directly.
6

Brand usage guidelines

Once you sign, you get a non-exclusive, revocable license to use the CivicLens name and logo to identify yourself as a partner. Keep it classy.

Do

  • ✓ Use "CivicLens Referral Partner" / "Solution Partner" / "Strategic Partner" accurately based on your tier
  • ✓ Display our logo on your website partners page
  • ✓ Reference CivicLens in proposals, case studies, and RFP responses
  • ✓ Request the official partner badge asset from us
  • ✓ Describe the product accurately using our marketing copy

Don't

  • × Claim a higher tier than you hold
  • × Imply CivicLens endorses anything we did not endorse
  • × Use the CivicLens name in your company name, product name, or domain without written permission
  • × Modify our logo (colors, proportions, lockups)
  • × Make claims about the product we have not made ourselves (performance, compliance, certifications)
  • × Use the CivicLens brand after termination

Press releases: Joint press releases require written approval from both parties. Unilateral announcements should not imply an exclusive or strategic relationship unless you are a Strategic Partner.

White-label exception: Strategic Partners with an OEM arrangement can ship the product under their own brand with no CivicLens attribution, per the Strategic Partner Statement of Work.
