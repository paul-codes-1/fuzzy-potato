How we calculate

Transcription savings

Traditional transcription takes roughly 4x real-time for trained staff plus review time. CivicLens uses automated speech recognition and delivers within minutes. We estimate 5 staff-hours saved per meeting hour (transcription + review + formatting).

FOIA / records request savings

With a searchable full-text archive and structured data extraction, requests that previously required manual archive searches can be resolved in minutes. We estimate a 75% reduction in staff time per request.

Additional value not calculated

This estimate excludes harder-to-quantify benefits: reduced legal risk from better record-keeping, faster onboarding of new council members, improved public engagement, and time savings for council aides preparing briefings.
