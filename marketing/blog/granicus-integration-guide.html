The Complete Guide to Getting More from Your Granicus Investment

CL
CivicLens Team
Published March 15, 2026
| 12 min read

Granicus is the backbone of meeting video for over 5,500 government organizations. If your city uses Granicus, you have already invested in recording, hosting, and streaming your public meetings. But most municipalities are only using a fraction of what that video archive could offer. The recordings sit on the server, technically public but effectively unsearchable, untranscribed, and unstructured.

This guide explains how CivicLens works with your existing Granicus setup to add AI-powered transcription, structured data extraction, natural-language search, and conversational Q&A, all without changing your recording workflow or migrating a single file.

For IT Directors and City Administrators

CivicLens connects to Granicus via its public media URLs. There is no API key exchange, no data migration, and no modification to your Granicus configuration. Your Granicus contract and setup remain exactly as they are.

How the Integration Works

Granicus stores meeting video at predictable, publicly accessible URLs. Each meeting clip has a unique ID, and the video player, metadata, and associated documents (agendas, minutes) are all reachable through standard HTTP requests. CivicLens uses this existing infrastructure as its data source.

Here is what happens when CivicLens processes a meeting from your Granicus archive:

1

Discovery

CivicLens scans your Granicus host for available meeting clips. It identifies new meetings automatically and can be configured to process them on a schedule (e.g., twice daily on weekdays).

2

Audio Extraction

Meeting audio is extracted as a compact MP3 (48kbps, mono). A typical 3-hour meeting produces a file under 24MB. The original video remains untouched on Granicus.

3

AI Transcription

OpenAI Whisper transcribes the audio with timestamped segments, enabling deep-linking back to specific moments in the original Granicus video player.

4

Document Ingestion

Agendas and minutes linked from your Granicus clips are automatically downloaded and parsed. PDF documents are extracted with pdfplumber, with OCR fallback for scanned documents.

5

Structured Extraction

A two-pass AI system extracts structured data: votes and roll calls, financial items and dollar amounts, public comments, attendance records, appointments, and contentious items. This data powers vote tracking, financial analysis, and alerting features.

6

RAG Indexing

All content, summaries, transcripts, facts, agendas, and minutes, is chunked and embedded into a vector database. This enables natural-language Q&A across your entire meeting archive.

What This Unlocks for Your City

Once your Granicus archive is processed through CivicLens, your staff and residents gain capabilities that were previously impossible:

Natural-Language Search

Ask "What has the council discussed about water rates?" and get sourced answers spanning years of meetings, with timestamps linking to the Granicus video player.

Vote Tracking

Track how each council member votes on every motion, with roll-call details. Set alerts for specific members, topics, or vote outcomes.

Financial Monitoring

Every dollar amount discussed in council meetings is extracted and categorized: appropriations, contracts, grants, bond issues. Searchable and filterable.

Smart Alerts

Subscribe to topics, keywords, or council members. Get notified via email, Slack, or Teams when something relevant comes up in a meeting.

Technical Details

For IT directors evaluating the integration, here are the technical specifics:

Connection method HTTP/HTTPS to public Granicus media URLs
Granicus credentials required None. CivicLens accesses only publicly available media.
Configuration needed Granicus hostname and view ID (provided during onboarding)
Data storage Processed data stored in tenant-isolated environment. Original video remains on Granicus.
Processing time ~10 minutes per meeting (transcription + extraction + indexing)
Scheduling Configurable cron-based automation (default: 12pm and 8pm weekdays)
Compliance SOC 2 audit trail, FOIA export support, tenant data isolation

Processing Your Backlog

One of the most common questions from Granicus customers is: "Can you process our historical meetings?" The answer is yes. CivicLens can process your entire Granicus archive, going back as far as your recordings exist. The typical onboarding process looks like this:

Week 1: Connect to your Granicus host and process the most recent 3 months of meetings. Your team reviews the output and provides feedback on any meeting-body-specific terminology or formatting preferences.

Weeks 2-3: Process the full backlog. A city with 5 years of bi-weekly meetings (roughly 500 clips) can be fully processed in 2-3 days of continuous processing.

Week 3+: Automated scheduling takes over. New meetings are detected and processed within hours of being posted to Granicus.

Common Questions from Granicus Customers

Does this replace Granicus?

No. CivicLens sits on top of Granicus. Your video hosting, streaming, and recording workflow remain exactly the same. CivicLens adds intelligence and search capabilities to the content Granicus already hosts.

Do we need to change our Granicus configuration?

No changes needed. CivicLens only needs your Granicus hostname (e.g., yourcity.granicus.com) and the view ID for your meeting archive.

What if we switch away from Granicus?

CivicLens is designed to work with any video hosting platform. If you migrate, your processed data and search index remain intact. Only the discovery and download pipeline needs to be reconfigured.

How accurate is the AI transcription?

OpenAI Whisper achieves 95%+ accuracy on typical meeting audio. Proper nouns (council member names, local street names) may need occasional correction, which can be configured via a custom dictionary during onboarding.
