CivicLens Extends Granicus Integration with Real-Time Vote Tracking and AI Search

New roll-call extraction, financial item surfacing, and cross-meeting natural-language Q&A deepen the platform's read-only integration with Granicus-hosted archives.

[City, State] — April 10, 2026 — CivicLens today announced an expansion of its integration with Granicus, the video hosting and meeting management platform used by thousands of US local governments. The expansion adds automated roll-call vote extraction, dollar-amount-level financial item tracking, and a retrieval-augmented natural language query system — all built as a read-only layer on top of a city's existing Granicus view. No migration, no re-upload, and no changes to the clerk's workflow are required.

With the new capabilities, city staff, journalists, and residents can ask questions like "Which council members voted against the Oakwood rezoning?" or "What appropriations has the council made for parks maintenance this fiscal year?" and receive answers that cite specific meetings, timestamps, and speaker turns. Every answer links back to the exact second in the source Granicus video, enabling instant human verification.

"Granicus is already the system of record for thousands of cities. We're not asking anyone to rip and replace anything," said [CEO / Spokesperson placeholder], CivicLens. "CivicLens reads from what you already have and adds an intelligence layer on top. That's the only posture that respects how government IT actually works."

Under the hood, the platform uses OpenAI Whisper for transcription, GPT-4o for structured extraction of votes and financial items, Anthropic's Claude for narrative summarization, and ChromaDB for semantic search across the full meeting archive. The architecture is multi-tenant with per-city data isolation, tenant-scoped API keys, and an immutable audit log tracking every query and administrative action. SAML 2.0 single sign-on is available for Enterprise customers who federate through Okta, Microsoft Entra, or Google Workspace.

The extended Granicus integration is available today to all CivicLens customers at no additional charge. Cities interested in evaluating the platform on their own Granicus archive can request a 90-day pilot at civiclens.ai.

About CivicLens

CivicLens is a SaaS platform that transforms city council meeting videos into structured, searchable intelligence. By ingesting Granicus-hosted meeting recordings, the platform delivers AI-powered transcription, two-pass summarization, vote tracking, financial item extraction, and natural language Q&A — all within minutes of a meeting ending. CivicLens is built for city clerks, journalists, advocacy organizations, and engaged residents who need to know exactly what local government is doing without watching hours of video. Learn more at civiclens.ai.
