About CivicLens

Company boilerplate · Paste into articles verbatim

CivicLens is a SaaS platform that transforms city council meeting videos into structured, searchable intelligence. By ingesting Granicus-hosted meeting recordings, the platform delivers AI-powered transcription, two-pass summarization, vote tracking, financial item extraction, and natural language Q&A — all within minutes of a meeting ending. CivicLens is built for city clerks, journalists, advocacy organizations, and engaged residents who need to know exactly what local government is doing without watching hours of video. Learn more at civiclens.ai.
