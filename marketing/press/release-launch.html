CivicLens Launches to Make City Council Meetings Searchable and Accountable

New AI platform converts hours of Granicus-hosted meeting video into structured votes, financial records, and citation-backed summaries within minutes of a meeting ending.

[City, State] — April 10, 2026 — CivicLens, an early-stage civic technology company, today announced the public launch of its AI-powered city council meeting intelligence platform. The platform ingests video from Granicus — the dominant meeting-hosting system used by thousands of US local governments — and converts each meeting into searchable transcripts, structured vote records, extracted financial items, narrative summaries, and a natural-language Q&A interface that answers questions across the entire archive with cited, timestamped sources.

Local governments produce thousands of hours of public meeting video every year, but that content remains effectively inaccessible: fewer than two percent of residents in mid-size cities report watching recorded council meetings. CivicLens is built to close that gap without replacing any existing system. It reads from the Granicus platform a city already runs and layers AI-generated intelligence on top, turning raw video into data a journalist, advocacy group, clerk, or resident can actually query.

The platform uses a two-pass processing pipeline: OpenAI's Whisper model transcribes meeting audio with timestamped segments, GPT-4o extracts structured facts (votes, roll calls, appropriations, attendance, public comments), and Anthropic's Claude generates section-by-section narrative summaries from those extracted facts. Every claim in every summary links back to the exact moment in the source video, making verification instant. A retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) layer powered by ChromaDB enables cross-meeting queries such as "What has the council approved for affordable housing in the last year?"

"The goal of AI in local government isn't to replace humans — it's to make the existing record legible," said [CEO / Spokesperson placeholder], CivicLens. "Every council meeting is already public. CivicLens just finally makes that record usable by the people it's for."

CivicLens launches with pilot deployments across multiple US cities, including reference integrations with the City of Elk Grove, California and the Lexington-Fayette Urban County Government in Kentucky. The platform is offered in Starter, Pro, and Enterprise tiers, with a dedicated nonprofit plan available to verified 501(c)(3) advocacy organizations at a 30 percent discount. Individual journalists and newsrooms can access a multi-city read-only plan for cross-jurisdictional reporting.

City clerks, IT directors, journalists, and advocacy teams interested in evaluating CivicLens can request a demo at civiclens.ai or contact the team at hello@civiclens.io. Media inquiries should be directed to press@civiclens.io.

About CivicLens

CivicLens is a SaaS platform that transforms city council meeting videos into structured, searchable intelligence. By ingesting Granicus-hosted meeting recordings, the platform delivers AI-powered transcription, two-pass summarization, vote tracking, financial item extraction, and natural language Q&A — all within minutes of a meeting ending. CivicLens is built for city clerks, journalists, advocacy organizations, and engaged residents who need to know exactly what local government is doing without watching hours of video.
