See CivicLens in action

A 3-minute guided tour of the six workflows that save city clerks, council members, and engaged residents hours every week. No signup required.

Step 1 of 6

Browse every meeting, instantly

Every session from City Council, Planning Commission, and the Budget Committee lives on a single searchable index. Filter by body, date, or topic, or jump straight to a keyword.

Full-text search Every spoken word, agenda PDF, and minutes document is indexed. Partial matches highlight in context.
Topic chips Meetings are auto-tagged by GPT-4o-mini into 3–8 topics — no manual tagging required.
Pulsing card The highlighted card is what you'd click in the real app to drill into a meeting. That's step 2.

Step 2 of 6

Skim 3 hours in 30 seconds

Every meeting gets a structured overview: votes, financial items, public comments, and contentious agenda items — all linked to the exact moment in the video.

Tabbed view Overview, Transcript, Agenda, Minutes — pick your level of detail.
Timestamp deep-links Every cyan chip jumps the embedded video to that exact second. No more scrubbing.
One-click Q&A Scope a question to just this meeting — or zoom out to the whole archive.

Step 3 of 6

Ask in plain English

RAG-powered Q&A searches every meeting summary, transcript, agenda, and minutes document in your archive. Every answer comes with citations you can click back to the source.

Retrieval-augmented Questions search a vector index built from summaries, facts, transcripts, agendas, and minutes — five chunk types per meeting.
Every claim is sourced Numbered citation chips at the bottom link straight to the clip and timestamp that backs each fact.
Scoped filters Narrow by body (Council, Planning, Budget) or date range — great for topic-specific research.

Step 4 of 6

Follow the thread across meetings

Multi-turn chat remembers context. Start broad, then drill in. Switch models mid-conversation between OpenAI and Anthropic. All citations persist across turns.

Conversational memory "Who voted against it?" in turn 2 resolves to the ordinance from turn 1 — no re-typing.
Cross-meeting context Answers pull from multiple sessions at once, so you can follow a policy thread across months.
Pick your model Switch between GPT-4o and Claude Sonnet mid-thread. Your chat history carries over.

Step 5 of 6

Track every vote, set every alert

Search votes by ordinance, member, topic, or date. Set alert rules to get notified the moment council takes action on the issues you care about.

Structured roll calls Every vote is extracted into structured data: ayes, nays, abstentions, and individual member votes.
Flexible alerts Trigger on keywords, specific members, financial thresholds, or any combination. Delivery to email, Slack, Teams, or webhooks.
Historical member data Click any member to see their complete voting history across every meeting in the archive.

Step 6 of 6

Analytics and admin, at a glance

See how your team is using CivicLens: query volume, top questions, triggered alerts, and API usage — all in one dashboard.

Usage insights See who's asking what. Trends help you spot topics worth publishing proactively.
Alert history Every triggered alert is logged with source citations, so you can trace why it fired.
Export anytime Download analytics as CSV or pipe to your BI tool via the REST API.
