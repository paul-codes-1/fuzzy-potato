## Meeting Overview
- **Date, Time, and Type:** August 14, 2007, Urban County Council Work Session
- **Presiding Officer and Notable Attendees:** Mayor Newberry chaired the meeting. All council members were present.
- **High-Level Summary:** The meeting addressed various administrative and budgetary matters, including rezoning requests, budget amendments, and new business items. Key discussions involved the approval of budget amendments, authorization of various agreements and contracts, and the discussion of property tax rates. The council also discussed the management audit and the implications of the Meadowbrook Golf Course sale.

## Roll Call & Attendance
- **Present:** All council members were present.
- **Absent/Late:** None mentioned.

## Key Decisions & Votes
- **Rezoning Requests:** Several rezoning requests were placed on the docket for future public hearings or approvals without dissent.
- **Budget Amendments:** Approved unanimously.
- **New Business Items:** Approved with the exception of item 'S', which was tabled for further clarification.
- **Ad Valorem Property Tax Rates:** A motion to maintain prior year's property tax rates with a slight reduction in refuse collection rates was passed unanimously.

## Detailed Agenda Item Discussion
- **Rezoning/Docket Approval:** Discussed the need for public hearings on specific rezoning cases, including properties on Alexandria Drive and East High Street.
- **Budget Amendments:** Approved without dissent, indicating adjustments to the fiscal plan.
- **New Business Items:** Included authorizations for various agreements and contracts, such as change orders for construction projects and agreements with law enforcement agencies.
- **Ad Valorem Property Tax Rates:** Discussed by Jim Deaton and Connie Underwood, resulting in a decision to maintain most rates at prior levels with minor adjustments.

## Budget & Financial Items
- **Cadentown School Project:** Change orders authorized up to $10,000.
- **Gainesway Park Project:** Contract increased by $7,500.
- **Various Agreements:** Federal and state funds were allocated for law enforcement and community projects, including $7,930 for domestic violence programs and $8,216 for law enforcement overtime.

## Public Comments & Citizen Input
- **Number of Speakers:** None for agenda items; one for non-agenda items.
- **Topics Addressed:** A citizen raised concerns about a property dispute involving a neighbor's construction.

## Presentations & Reports
- **Management Audit:** Presented by Jerry Newfarmer and Julia Novak, outlining the scope and approach for reviewing government operations.
- **Ad Valorem Property Tax Rates:** Presented by Jim Deaton, discussing the need for maintaining or adjusting current rates.

## Appointments & Recognitions
- **None discussed.**

## Controversies & Disagreements
- **Employee Picnic and Half-Day Off:** Discussion on the cancellation of the annual employee picnic and half-day off, with some council members expressing concerns about the decision and its timing.

## Action Items & Follow-ups
- **Management Audit:** Continued engagement with council members and staff for input.
- **Meadowbrook Golf Course:** Referred to the Services Committee for further exploration of alternatives.

## Notable Quotes
1. "Our goal is to do a physical exam of the government, see what people do, and compare it with best practices." - Jerry Newfarmer
2. "We need to maintain the prior year's property tax rates, with the exception of refuse collection rates." - Council Member Lane
3. "The Meadowbrook Golf Course is not much of a golf course, but it provides green space that we need to consider preserving." - Council Member Stephens

## Implications for Residents
- **Property Tax Rates:** The decision to maintain most property tax rates at prior levels should provide stability for residents.
- **Public Safety and Community Projects:** Federal and state funding allocations for law enforcement and community projects aim to enhance public safety and services.
- **Meadowbrook Golf Course:** The potential sale and development of this property could impact local green space and recreational opportunities.
