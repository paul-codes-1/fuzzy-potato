## Meeting Overview
- **Date and Time**: August 14, 2007, 1:00 PM
- **Type of Meeting**: Services Committee Meeting
- **Presiding Officer**: Chair CM Ellinger
- **Notable Attendees**: CM Myers, CM James, CM Blues, CM DeCamp, CM Beard, CM Crosbie, CM McChord, CM Blevins, CM Lane
- **Summary**: The Services Committee discussed several key issues including traffic control measures, Sunday alcohol sales, and transportation programs for seniors. Notable decisions included the approval of a multi-way stop at Headley Ave/6th Street and the removal of several items from the committee agenda. Discussions on Sunday alcohol sales were extensive but deferred for further consideration.

## Roll Call & Attendance
- **Present**: All committee members were in attendance.
- **Absent**: None
- **Late Arrivals**: None

## Key Decisions & Votes
1. **Multi-Way Stop at Headley Ave/6th Street**
   - **Outcome**: Passed unanimously
   - **Context**: Requested by the Northside Neighborhood Association to improve safety. Traffic studies showed near-qualifying conditions for a stop.

2. **Removal of Panhandling Item from Committee**
   - **Outcome**: Passed unanimously
   - **Context**: The issue had been resolved and no longer required committee attention.

3. **Removal of Multi-Way Stop at Kastle and Montclair and Sump Pump Redirect Program**
   - **Outcome**: Passed unanimously
   - **Context**: The neighborhood decided against the stop, and issues with the sump pump program were resolved.

## Detailed Agenda Item Discussion
1. **Alleghany Ave Traffic Concerns**
   - **Discussion**: CM Blevins presented issues related to traffic patterns affecting Rosa Parks Elementary School. Proposed solutions included allowing left turns and improving pedestrian crossings.
   - **Outcome**: Directed traffic engineering to proceed with proposed changes, allocating $86,618 from the fiscal year 08 fund balance.

2. **Sunday Alcohol Sales**
   - **Discussion**: Extensive debate on potential changes to allow broader Sunday alcohol sales. CM Ellinger, CM Dr. Stevens, and others discussed legislative changes and community impacts.
   - **Outcome**: Issue left in committee for further information and draft ordinance circulation.

3. **Taxi Ordinance and ITN Program**
   - **Discussion**: Gail Reese updated on the ITN program for senior transportation. Legal and regulatory challenges at the state level were highlighted.
   - **Outcome**: Request for council support in addressing state-level legal barriers.

## Budget & Financial Items
- **Traffic Engineering Project**: $86,618 allocated for traffic improvements at Alleghany Ave.

## Public Comments & Citizen Input
- **Speakers**: Robo Sutherland (Northside Neighborhood Association), Anna Marletta (Bellini’s), Renee Jackson (Downtown Lexington Corp), Gene McLean (Frankfort Lobbyist), Jason Baird (Frankfort Lobbyist)
- **Topics**: Support for multi-way stop, Sunday alcohol sales, and ITN program.

## Presentations & Reports
- **Traffic Study Report**: Presented by Brad Frazier, Traffic Engineering, regarding the Headley Ave/6th Street multi-way stop.

## Appointments & Recognitions
- **None discussed**

## Controversies & Disagreements
- **Sunday Alcohol Sales**: Divided opinions on expanding sales, with concerns about social impacts and comparisons to other cities.

## Action Items & Follow-ups
- **Sunday Alcohol Sales**: Await further draft ordinance and additional research.
- **ITN Program**: Seek legal assistance and state-level advocacy for implementation.

## Notable Quotes
1. "We need to keep an eye on this... to ensure we're not creating an unsafe situation." - CM DeCamp on Alleghany Ave traffic changes.
2. "Liquor should not be a tourist attraction." - CM James on Sunday alcohol sales.
3. "We have a responsibility as legislators... to safeguard the community." - CM James on alcohol legislation.

## Implications for Residents
- **Traffic Improvements**: Enhanced safety and traffic flow around Rosa Parks Elementary School.
- **Alcohol Sales**: Potential changes could align Lexington with other cities, impacting local businesses and community norms.
- **Senior Transportation**: Progress on ITN could improve mobility for seniors, pending state-level legal adjustments.
